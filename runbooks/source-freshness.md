# Source freshness

1. Identify the source, effective period, capture date, tier, and claims that depend on it.
2. Mark stale or superseded evidence explicitly; do not silently delete historical claims.
3. Acquire only authorized NFL/team/public sources and retain citation location.
4. Reconcile conflicts by source hierarchy or human review.
5. Re-run affected scouting, analytics, and game-plan evaluations before publication.

The default connector enforces HTTPS, the registered domain allowlist, a bounded response size, a finite timeout, and redirect containment. A failed or redirected source remains explicit evidence and must not be promoted silently.
