# Incident escalation

1. Contain the affected feature flag, source, tenant, or release candidate.
2. Preserve observability events, permission denials, audit history, and relevant inputs.
3. Notify the program owner and qualified security/football/safety owner as applicable.
4. Do not make medical, clearance, officiating, or unauthorized competitive-intelligence decisions.
5. Record the decision, remediation, verification, and release approval.
