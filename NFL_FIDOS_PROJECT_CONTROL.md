## Overall project goal

Build an NFL-exclusive Football Intelligence & Development Operating System that helps professional football organizations teach, learn, design, prepare, practice, analyze, evaluate, game-plan, and continuously improve. It must understand football globally, the team specifically, the opponent contextually, and each player or coach at the appropriate level of instruction and analysis.

The completed system must close this loop for both NFL players and coaches/staff:

1. **What do we know?** Rules, doctrine, team system, personnel, opponent, film, analytics, history, and evidence.
2. **What should we do?** Scheme, play, adjustment, practice, development, matchup, game plan, and game-management recommendations.
3. **How do we teach it?** Diagrams, animation, film, explanations, drills, practice, quizzes, playbooks, and mastery pathways.
4. **Did it work?** Film grades, assignment and technique evaluation, analytics, development, self-scout, outcomes, and continuous improvement.

## Locked project constraints

- League scope is **NFL only** unless the owner approves a scope change.
- The project is staged; the full destination does not authorize building every feature immediately.
- Stage 0 discovery and dependency architecture must precede detailed subsystem design and production implementation.
- Football must be modeled compositionally; scheme labels alone are not complete schemes.
- Recommendations must preserve context, sample size, assumptions, evidence, uncertainty, and alternative interpretations.
- Every material recommendation must distinguish fact, rule, team rule, observed tendency, coaching preference, contextual principle, and hypothesis.
- General-purpose agents cannot override authoritative rules, team rules, locked playbook assignments, validated terminology, permissions, or human authority.
- Specialist agents are callable roles, not automatically permanent running processes.
- Material changes to scope, terminology, schemas, agent responsibility, doctrine, or architecture require impact review and roadmap update.
